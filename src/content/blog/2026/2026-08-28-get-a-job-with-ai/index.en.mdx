I spent the past 3 months looking for a new professional challenge, and used it as an opportunity to test how AI could help me in my job search. Finally, I prepared a talk for "[ANFAIA](https://anfaia.org)'s summer talks" to help mentees understand a bit more about how the hiring process works in the tech industry and how AI can help them get a job.

Here's the recording of the talk (in Spanish).

::youtube[]{id=ivsu42LBU6g}


You can also find the slides of the talk [here](https://u.pcloud.link/publink/show?code=XZlP9JJZt0luzkFdvTkj8sONAJE3S4NNOglV).


During the talk, I mentioned multiple agents I left here for you.

> These agents were optimized for my processes and CV. I shared them for reference purposes and as examples, but note that they may not work without a structured CV.

### position-finder

Finds remote roles that fit a candidate's CV

::codefrom[]{path=./assets/position-finder.md}


### company-research

Researches the company's stage, market, challenges, etc.

::codefrom[]{path=./assets/company-research.md}

### cv-tailor

Selects and highlights the relevant parts of the candidate's CV for the position

::codefrom[]{path=./assets/cv-tailor.md}

### ats-specialist

Checks the CV from an ATS pre-filter perspective

::codefrom[]{path=./assets/ats-specialist.md}

### cv-adversary

Finds the gaps that could keep you from being hired, and provides feedback on how to address them where possible.

::codefrom[]{path=./assets/cv-adversary.md}

I hope you find the talk and the agent definitions useful.
